Vietnamese Pho Bowls

Flavorful, Simple, and Delicious!

Jump to Recipe

Vietnamese Pho is a noodle soup that's filled with protein and warms you up every mouthful!

Why it's special..

Homemade Pho allows you to have complete control over flavors and enjoy a healthy meal anytime.

My family and I love to try new foods, and we immediately fell in love with Pho the first time trying it.

Ingredients

Broth 6 cups
Ginger 4 fresh slices
Pho Broth Concentrate 3 packages
Pho noodles 16 ounces
Protein (cooked chicken, beef, tofu, mushrooms, thinly sliced steak, or shrimp) 3 ounces
Thinly sliced green onion, chilli, lime, or vegetables to your liking
Cilantro 8 sprigs cut
Bean sprouts 4 ounces
Thai basil 4 sprigs cut

Instructions

Step 1

Combine broth, pho seasoning, and ginger in a medium pot. Let it simmer after being brought to a boil.

Step 2

As broth simmers, place Pho noodles in a mixing bowl. Boil additional water to pour over noodles to soak and soften for 10 minutes. Drain.

Step 3

Divide noodles into multiple bowls, then pour the hot broth and add protein. Garnish and add additional sauces if desired.

Enjoy homemade Pho to serve your family and friends on any day!
